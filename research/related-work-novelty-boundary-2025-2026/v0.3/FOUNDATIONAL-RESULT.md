# Foundational Novelty Pressure v0.3 — bounded result

## Executive result

```text
SNE_HEC                        -> DEFEATED_AS_FOUNDATIONAL_NOVELTY_CANDIDATE
COMMON_NON_EFFECTS             -> DEFEATED_AS_FOUNDATIONAL_NOVELTY_CANDIDATE
AVAILABLE_CONSIDERED_AUTHORITY -> MATERIALLY_NARROWED
TYPED_CONTINUITY               -> DEFEATED_AS_FOUNDATIONAL_NOVELTY_CANDIDATE
CROSS_DOMAIN_REUSE             -> MATERIALLY_NARROWED

survives_foundational_pressure -> 0
novelty_established            -> false
```

This is a successful negative research result. It does not invalidate Matawaka as an engineering or research program. It changes what the defensible contribution can be.

## 1. SNE-HEC is not a foundational novelty claim

Secure information-flow models from the 1970s onward already formalize noninterference and permitted flows. Decentralized IFC adds explicit authority/declassification. Proof-Carrying Authorization requires a valid proof before a protected operation. Policy-composition calculi reason about interactions among enforcement policies. Provenance semirings provide a formal algebra for provenance composition.

Therefore a broad principle such as:

`valid evidence must not silently acquire stronger semantic power through composition`

is best treated as a modern AI-governance application/integration of established formal ideas, not a newly invented foundational logic.

Potential research value moves to a **semantic-escalation fault taxonomy, benchmark and conformance method** specialized to heterogeneous AI evidence/governance surfaces.

## 2. `non_effects` is an engineering protocol convention, not a new primitive

Effect systems have made effects first-class static semantics since the 1980s. Frame/local reasoning formally specifies what an operation may touch while preserving the rest. Contemporary v0.2 pressure had already found machine-readable negative verification fields before Matawaka.

A shared `non_effects` field remains useful: it can make verifier limitations explicit and machine-checkable. But the foundational idea that specifications should constrain effects/non-effects is old.

The research question should therefore be empirical/interoperability-oriented:

> Does a uniform negative-semantics envelope measurably prevent semantic overclaim when heterogeneous AI evidence is composed or displayed?

## 3. PoAI availability/consideration survives only as an operational synthesis candidate

Foundational work already separates:

- awareness from belief;
- an awareness set from the subset seriously considered;
- beliefs/goals/intentions/actions;
- beliefs/obligations/intentions/desires;
- plan reliance, responsibility and delegated authority.

Thus the individual distinctions are not new.

The remaining candidate is narrower and operational:

> one machine-auditable record for heterogeneous intelligence resources that separately binds provenance, existence, decision-specific availability, consideration/reliance, authority and responsibility.

Whether even this combined record is new remains unresolved. It requires a dedicated search of decision-provenance, knowledge-management, scientific workflow and regulated decision-record systems.

## 4. Seven-layer continuity is an architectural synthesis, not foundational novelty

Older agent theory, authorization logic, W3C PROV and in-toto collectively cover the semantic territory of epistemic state, intention, obligation/responsibility, authority/delegation, expected action steps, performed activities and observed artifact/outcome lineage.

The exact Matawaka sequence may still be coherent and useful:

`State/Evidence -> Availability -> Intent -> Authority/Responsibility -> Coordination -> Action -> Outcome/Successor`

but choosing these seven boundaries does not establish a new foundational logic. Its justification should come from **ablation evidence**:

- what fails when Availability is collapsed into State;
- what fails when Intent is collapsed into Authority;
- what fails when Responsibility is collapsed into Authority;
- what fails when Coordination is treated as Permission;
- what fails when Permit is treated as performed Action;
- what fails when Action is treated as Outcome/Truth.

That can become a strong empirical protocol contribution without a world-first architecture claim.

## 5. Cross-domain reuse is strongly narrowed by OPM/W3C PROV

Open Provenance Model and W3C PROV are explicitly domain-neutral. PROV represents people, organizations and software agents; plans as intended steps toward goals; responsibility for activities; delegation of authority/responsibility; and activity/entity provenance.

So Matawaka cannot claim to have invented a common abstraction spanning human and software activity or documents and actions.

The narrower unresolved contribution is:

> one practical profile that adds decision-time epistemics and fail-closed pre-action authority to domain-neutral provenance, then demonstrates unchanged semantics across accountable human-AI intellectual work and autonomous consequential actions.

This remains an integration/interoperability hypothesis, not foundational novelty.

## Revised contribution posture

After v0.1 -> v0.2 -> v0.3, the strongest research posture is:

### Not defensible as novelty

- new theory of non-escalating composition;
- new concept of negative effects/non-claims;
- new distinction between awareness and consideration;
- new separation of intention, obligation/authority, action and outcome;
- new domain-neutral provenance model;
- new multi-layer governance architecture merely because seven layers are named.

### Still potentially defensible as research contribution

1. **Semantic-Escalation Fault Taxonomy** for AI-governance systems: a precise catalogue of invalid promotions such as evidence->truth, availability->intent, identity->authority, review->permission, permit->action, action->outcome/causality.
2. **Adversarial Conformance Corpus / Benchmark** that tests real agent/provenance systems for those promotions.
3. **Cross-domain integration profile** combining decision epistemics + authority/responsibility + action admission + outcome provenance on top of established formal foundations.
4. **Empirical ablation evidence** for which semantic boundaries materially prevent failures in AI-agent workflows.
5. **Interoperability mapping** that reuses C2PA/CAWG/PROV/SCITT/authorization mechanisms without laundering their evidence into stronger meanings.

The likely scientific paper should therefore shift from claiming a new foundational logic to testing a new falsifiable systems hypothesis:

> AI-governance failures can be systematically modeled as semantic-escalation faults, and explicit typed boundaries plus adversarial non-promotion tests can reduce those faults across otherwise heterogeneous provenance, authorization and agent-action systems.

## Next gate

Before treating even the taxonomy/benchmark as new, run a separate landscape audit for:

- type-confusion and privilege-escalation taxonomies;
- authorization-bypass and confused-deputy benchmarks;
