## Attribution and anti-impersonation

When material from this repository is reused under an applicable license, attribution should preserve the project name, canonical repository and the attribution required by that license.

A downstream work should not represent itself as the canonical UU-AAP repository, an official successor, or an official position of the project unless that status has been explicitly established through the canonical governance process.

Removal of provenance references does not erase the historical repository record from which the material originated.
