# Issue proposal: Separate correction, dispute and appeal

The draft distinguishes:

- correction by a responsible actor;
- dispute by a third party;
- appeal against a disposition or process.

Please provide cases where this model fails, especially where:

- reviewers disagree;
- a registry acts unfairly;
- evidence must remain private;
- a correction should not erase reputational harm;
- an anonymous or pseudonymous challenger has legitimate evidence.
