# Community Conduct

UU-AAP discussion is expected to be rigorous and adversarial toward ideas, not people.

Participants SHOULD:

- criticize claims and mechanisms with evidence;
- disclose conflicts of interest when materially relevant;
- distinguish technical objections from moral preference;
- allow uncertainty and disagreement to remain visible;
- avoid shaming people for using or not using AI;
- avoid demands for unnecessary disclosure of private creative material.

Harassment, doxxing, threats and targeted disclosure of private prompt histories or personal information are not acceptable forms of protocol review.
