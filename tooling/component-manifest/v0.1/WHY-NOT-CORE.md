# Why Component Manifest is not a Core primitive

Component Manifest solves engineering discovery, dependency and conformance orchestration. It does not add a new epistemic, intentional, authority, coordination, execution or outcome transition.

Therefore it belongs in reusable tooling rather than the seven-primitive semantic Core.

Promotion would require separate evidence that tooling/profile placement is insufficient, at least two independent product consumers, cross-product conformance, migration semantics and a distinct architectural decision.
