# Privacy Governance Evidence Closure v0.1

This slice closes the internal repository-evidence gap for privacy without claiming independent privacy certification or legal compliance.

It binds existing PSR/PEF and Circumstantial Provenance evidence to an explicit governance receipt covering payload minimization, commitment/reference sufficiency, default denial of cross-context correlation, and the absence of required profiling/total-history semantics.

`Repository Privacy Evidence != Privacy Certification`

`Data Minimization Evidence != Universal Downstream Compliance`

`Correlation Default Denial != Identity Resolution Authority`

The receipt creates no release authority, identity-resolution authority, profile-construction authority, external effect, certification, or legal status.
