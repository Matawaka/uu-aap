# Contributing

Contributions are welcome from authors, publishers, librarians, technologists, researchers, readers, accessibility specialists, legal experts and AI providers.

## Preferred contribution style

A strong proposal contains:

1. a concrete problem or failure case;
2. the affected clause or data object;
3. a proposed change;
4. privacy/security impact;
5. interoperability impact;
6. alternative positions.

## Especially useful contributions

- real publishing workflows;
- cases involving multiple authors;
- cases involving confidential prompts or sources;
- dispute and correction scenarios;
- C2PA mappings;
- W3C VC attestation examples;
- accessibility and internationalization feedback;
- negative test vectors.

## Out of scope as proof

Please do not rely on:

- unsupported claims that linguistic AI detection establishes authorship;
- mandatory full-prompt disclosure without a threat model;
- human/AI percentage scores as a general authorship metric;
- cryptographic integrity as proof of factual truth.

## Contribution licensing — inbound = outbound

The repository uses the licensing map in `LICENSE.md`.

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion in this repository, you represent that you are authorized to submit and license that material and agree that, if incorporated, the contribution may be distributed under the applicable outbound license for that material:

- **non-software content:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC-BY-4.0`);
- **software and implementation content:** Apache License 2.0 (`Apache-2.0`).

For software contributions, Apache License 2.0 Section 5 also applies to intentional submissions unless you explicitly state otherwise or a separate written agreement applies.

For non-software contributions, intentional submission for inclusion constitutes permission to incorporate and distribute that contribution under CC BY 4.0, unless you clearly state different terms before acceptance and the maintainers explicitly accept those terms.

If you do **not** intend material to be contributed, mark it conspicuously as **`Not a Contribution`** or otherwise state that restriction clearly before it is incorporated.

The project currently does not require a separate contributor license agreement (CLA). A future governance change may introduce one only through an explicit public policy change.

## Rights and provenance responsibility

Do not submit material you do not have authority to license under the applicable terms.

If your contribution contains third-party material, identify it and its applicable license or permission. Inclusion in this repository does not silently relicense third-party material.

AI assistance does not transfer contribution responsibility. A submitter remains responsible for the representations they make about the material they intentionally contribute and for disclosing known licensing restrictions relevant to acceptance.

`AI assistance != responsibility transfer`

`merge authority != authorship of every contribution`

Accepted contribution provenance should remain attributable through Git history, issues, pull requests, review records or other repository evidence. Merge does not erase the contributor's recorded provenance.

## Canonical-status boundary

Submitting, reviewing, implementing or forking repository material does not by itself confer canonical project status, endorsement or successor authority.

`fork != canonical successor`

`open license != endorsement`

The canonical project lineage and anti-impersonation boundary are described in `NOTICE.md` and repository governance records.
