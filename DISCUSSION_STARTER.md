# Discussion: What should accountable AI-augmented authorship mean?

**Live discussion:** https://github.com/Matawaka/uu-aap/discussions/8

This file preserves the initial public discussion prompt as part of the protocol's own provenance history.

Hello. I am publishing UU-AAP v0.1 as an open proposal, not as a finished answer.

The project starts from one assumption: the useful question is not “How many words did AI type?” but “Who governed the meaning, who selected and rejected alternatives, what evidence exists, where does uncertainty remain, and who accepts responsibility for which decisions?”

I would like the first discussion to challenge that assumption.

In particular:

- Where does human agency become too weak to call the process human-governed?
- Can responsibility be represented fairly without making the visible author responsible for an entire AI supply chain?
- How much provenance is useful before transparency becomes surveillance?
- Should concept lineage be part of the core protocol?
- What must remain private even in the strongest review profile?
- How should a third party dispute a provenance claim without turning the registry into a reputation court?

Please bring counterexamples. A good failure case is more valuable at this stage than agreement.

— Matawaka, initial proposer/editor
