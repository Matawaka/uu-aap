# References and Adjacent Work

UU-AAP is intended to compose with, not replace, established provenance and credential standards.

## C2PA

- C2PA Guiding Principles: https://c2pa.org/principles/
- C2PA Content Credentials / Technical Specification 2.4: https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/C2PA_Specification.html
- Content Credentials format guidance: https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/ContentCredentials.html

Relevant relationship: C2PA provides verifiable provenance assertions, artifact binding, signatures and repository receipts. UU-AAP adds domain-specific claims about authorship governance, AI roles, responsibility scopes, concept lineage, uncertainty and contestability.

## W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0

- Recommendation announcement: https://www.w3.org/news/2025/the-verifiable-credentials-2-0-family-of-specifications-is-now-a-w3c-recommendation/
- Data model: https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/

Relevant relationship: reviewer, publisher or organizational attestations may be represented as portable verifiable claims.

## NIST AI RMF

- AI Risk Management Framework: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- Generative AI Profile: https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

Relevant relationship: governance, transparency, documentation, human oversight and risk management inform implementation policy, but UU-AAP is not a risk-management framework.

## IETF Proof of Process drafts — adjacent Internet-Drafts

- Proof of Process Evidence Framework: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-condrey-rats-pop/
- Architecture and Evidence Format (`draft-condrey-rats-pop-protocol-06`): https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-condrey-rats-pop-protocol/

As checked on 22 August 2026, the general `draft-condrey-rats-pop-01` had an expiry date of 15 August 2026, while `draft-condrey-rats-pop-protocol-06` lists 22 August 2026. These documents MUST be treated only as adjacent experimental/work-in-progress material, not as an IETF standard or endorsement.

Relevant difference: PoP explores attestation of human-driven creation process and, in some drafts, stronger evidence of physical/behavioral authorship. UU-AAP intentionally does not require biometric or continuous-process evidence and focuses on declared governance, scoped responsibility, uncertainty and contestability.

## Citation warning

Internet-Drafts are temporary documents that may expire, be replaced or be withdrawn. They MUST NOT be represented as established IETF standards.
