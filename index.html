UU-AAP

Augmented Authorship & Accountability Protocol

Human agency, not human purity. Provenance of decisions, not surveillance of typing.

UU-AAP is an open proposal for transparent and contestable human–AI authorship. It records governance of meaning, AI participation, scoped responsibility, evidence, uncertainty, artifact integrity and version history.

Not an AI detector.
UU-AAP does not score “human purity” and does not require continuous surveillance or complete prompt disclosure.
Responsibility is scoped.
Intent, concept selection, factual verification, editorial approval, publication and technical integrity can belong to different actors.
Four profiles.
D — Disclosed · T — Traceable · V — Verifiable · R — Reviewed
Pilot 001 — «Вайбкодинг реальности».
The first real-work UU-AAP/T manifest is now public. It records substantial AI participation, human governing authority, selective disclosure and explicit limitations without pretending that a canonical ebook artifact is already cryptographically bound.
