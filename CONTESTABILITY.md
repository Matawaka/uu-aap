# Contestability, Corrections and Appeal

UU-AAP treats provenance claims as contestable records, not final judgments.

## 1. Correction

A correction is initiated by an actor responsible for the original or successor manifest.

A correction MUST create a successor record when the changed field is material to conformance, responsibility, provenance, review or verification.

## 2. Dispute

A dispute is a third-party challenge to a specific claim or scope.

A registry SHOULD preserve:

- target manifest/claim;
- challenger identifier;
- challenge statement;
- evidence references;
- response from affected parties;
- current status;
- disposition rationale.

## 3. Appeal

An appeal challenges the disposition process or outcome rather than merely restating the original disagreement.

A future profile SHOULD support independent appeal when a registry or certifier makes consequential decisions.

## 4. No silent erasure

The existence of a successful correction does not require deleting the historical claim. User interfaces SHOULD make the corrected current state prominent while preserving audit history.

## 5. Right to unresolved disagreement

A dispute MAY remain unresolved. The protocol does not require forced consensus.

## 6. Anti-retaliation principle

Conformance implementations SHOULD NOT treat the act of filing a good-faith correction, dispute or appeal as negative reputation evidence by itself.
